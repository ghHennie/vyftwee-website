# Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to source, deliver and invoice your part, and we never share your details with suppliers.

Vyf Twee (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Our Information Officer is Hennie Botha, reachable at admin@vyftwee.co.za.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Information | Why we need it |
| --- | --- |
| Your WhatsApp number and name | To communicate with you about your request |
| Photo of your licence disc, and the vehicle details from it (make, model, year, VIN, engine number, registration) | To identify the correct part for your vehicle |
| The part you need and the part types you would accept | To ask suppliers for quotes |
| Delivery address and contact details | To arrange delivery by courier |
| Your decisions, payment status and invoice | To complete and record the sale |

Our websites use no cookies, analytics or tracking, and load nothing from third parties.

## 2. Who we share it with

| Recipient | What they receive | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suppliers | The vehicle details and the part, never your name, number or address | To quote and supply the part |
| The courier | Your name, number and delivery address | To deliver the part |
| PayFast | The payment amount and the details you enter on PayFast's pages | To process your payment |
| Meta (WhatsApp) | Our messages with you | WhatsApp carries the conversation |
| Microsoft Azure (South Africa) | Your request records | Where our platform stores data |
| Google Maps | Your delivery address | To confirm the address and location |

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

## 3. How long we keep it

Invoices and payment records are kept for five years, as tax law requires. Other request records are kept only as long as they are needed for the purposes above.

## 4. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by contacting our Information Officer. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator (www.inforegulator.org.za).

## 5. Opting out

Reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time and we will send you no further messages. Sending us a new message starts a conversation again.
