# Cancellation Policy

You can cancel at any point before you accept the part without paying anything; after payment, the seven-day cooling-off right applies.

## 1. Cancelling by you

| When you cancel | What it costs you |
| --- | --- |
| Before you accept a quote | Nothing. Simply do not accept it |
| After accepting the quote, before the part is collected | Nothing. Let us know on WhatsApp as soon as possible |
| While the part is on its way | Nothing. We return the part to the supplier at our cost |
| At delivery, by rejecting the part | Nothing. You only pay for a part you accept |
| After paying | Nothing. Within seven days of delivery, without a reason, we collect the part at no cost to you and refund you in full (see the Refund Policy) |

To cancel, send a WhatsApp message to +27 79 525 5987 or an email to admin@vyftwee.co.za with your request number.

## 2. Cancelling by us

We may cancel a request or an order, without cost to you, if the part is no longer available, the supplier withdraws its quote, or delivery is not possible on the agreed date and a new date cannot be agreed. If you have already paid, we refund you in full.

## 3. Quotes that expire

A quote that is not accepted within 48 hours lapses automatically. You may ask us for a new quote.
