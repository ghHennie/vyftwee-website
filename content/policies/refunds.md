# Refund Policy

You inspect every part before you pay, so most problems are settled before any money changes hands; after payment, the law and the warranties below protect you.

## 1. Inspection before payment

When the courier delivers, check the part. If it is wrong, damaged or not as described, reject it: you pay nothing, and we return it to the supplier at our cost.

## 2. Seven-day cooling-off

You may cancel your purchase within seven days after delivery, without giving a reason. The part must be unused and in the condition you received it. We arrange for the courier to collect it at no cost to you, and refund the full amount within 30 days of your cancellation, using the method you paid with.

## 3. Warranties by part type

| Part type | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| All parts | The Consumer Protection Act's six-month warranty against defects |
| New (OEM, original manufacturer) | The manufacturer's warranty, generally 12 months, subject to the manufacturer's conditions |
| Aftermarket | The brand's warranty, where it is longer than six months |
| Reconditioned and used | The six-month warranty, judged against the part's used condition; defects visible and accepted at inspection are excluded |

Warranties do not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, incorrect fitting, or a part used on a vehicle other than the one it was ordered for. Some manufacturers require their parts to be bought from or fitted by their dealers for their own warranty to apply.

## 4. Making a warranty claim

1. Contact us on WhatsApp or by email with your invoice number, a description of the fault and, if possible, photos.
2. Keep proof that a qualified mechanic fitted the part.
3. We will arrange to collect the part and assess it with the supplier.
4. If the part is defective, we will repair or replace it or refund you, as the Consumer Protection Act provides.

## 5. Refunds

Refunds are paid to the card or account you paid from, normally within ten working days of our decision, and no later than the time limits set by law.
