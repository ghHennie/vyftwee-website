2. Our service

We source new, reconditioned and used vehicle parts from independent suppliers, arrange delivery by courier, and sell the part to you once you accept it. We currently serve clients in Pretoria.

3. How an order works

  1. You send a photo of your vehicle's licence disc to our WhatsApp number and complete the request form: the part, the part types you would accept, your contact details and your delivery address.
  2. We ask our suppliers for quotes and send you a quote with the total price.
  3. If you accept the quote, we agree a delivery date and time with you and arrange the courier.
  4. The courier collects the part from the supplier and delivers it to you.
  5. You inspect the part. If you accept it, you pay; if you reject it, you pay nothing.

4. When the sale happens

The part stays the supplier's until you accept it at inspection. At that moment Vyf Twee buys it from the supplier and sells it to you. If you reject the part, no sale takes place and we return it to the supplier at our cost. The supplier's identity is not shared with you.

5. Prices

The quote shows the total you will pay, including the part and delivery, in South African rand. Vyf Twee is not a registered VAT vendor, so no VAT is charged on our service and we cannot issue tax invoices. Where a supplier is a VAT vendor, its VAT forms part of the part price. A quote is valid for 48 hours and subject to availability: if the supplier no longer has the part, we will tell you and the quote lapses at no cost to you.

6. Payment

You pay only after you have inspected and accepted the part, through a secure PayFast payment link at handover with the courier. Card details are entered on PayFast's pages and are never seen or stored by us. You receive an invoice by WhatsApp once payment is confirmed.
