52 Car Parts

Vehicle parts sourced for you and delivered to your door. You inspect the part first and pay only if you accept it.

What you buy

We source new, reconditioned and used vehicle parts from independent suppliers and deliver them to you by courier. We currently serve clients in Pretoria.

52 Car Parts is a brand of Vyf Twee (Pty) Ltd. When you buy a part through 52 Car Parts, you buy it from Vyf Twee (Pty) Ltd.

How it works

  1. Send a photo of your vehicle's licence disc to our WhatsApp number and complete the request form.
  2. We ask our suppliers for quotes and send you a quote with the total price, including delivery.
  3. If you accept the quote, we agree a delivery date and time with you.
  4. A courier collects the part from the supplier and delivers it to you.
  5. You inspect the part. If you accept it, you pay through a secure PayFast payment link at handover. If you reject it, you pay nothing.

Quotes

A quote is valid for 48 hours and subject to availability. If the supplier no longer has the part, we will tell you and the quote lapses at no cost to you.
